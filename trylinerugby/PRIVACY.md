# Privacy: Try Line Rugby

Try Line Rugby is made to be safe for children. It collects nothing about you or your child.

## What the game keeps

Settings, the teams you make, your championship and your Trophy Cabinet are saved on this device only: in the browser's storage under the name `tryline.v1`, or in the app's own storage. Anything you type, such as a team name, stays there too. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data for the game's site, removes it.

## What the game does not do

- No ads.
- No analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to contact other players.
- No purchases.
- It never asks for your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

## Going online

Once loaded, the game plays offline. When it is online it fetches only its own files, from the site it came from, to load and to update itself.

The web version also loads its two typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives that request, as it does from any website that uses its fonts. The game adds nothing about you or your play to it.

## Import

Import is in the App Store version only. A grown-up check comes first. Import then fetches the one club or tournament page you paste, on your device, and keeps only the team and competition details it needs, such as team names and fixtures. It never keeps any person's name, photo or contact details, and it sends nothing to anyone.

## The grown-up check

Anything that leaves the game, such as installing it or importing a club page, first asks a question a young child cannot answer.
