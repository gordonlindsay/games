# Privacy: Tryline Rugby Sevens

Tryline Rugby Sevens is made to be safe for children. It collects nothing about you or your child. Nobody, including us, receives your name, your email, where you are or how you play.

Last updated 8 November 2026. This covers the app from the App Store, the Android app once it is on Google Play, and the game on the web.

## What the game keeps

Settings, the teams you make, your championship, your Trophy Cabinet, your career (including the optional first name you give your player), any league you import and the unlocks you have bought are saved on this device only: in the browser's storage under the name `tryline.v1`, or in the app's own storage. Anything you type, such as a team name, stays there too. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's data for the game's site, removes it.

## What the game does not do

- No ads.
- No analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to contact other players.
- No subscriptions.
- It never asks for your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

## Purchases

The app is free to download. Full Game and Super Fan are one-off unlocks, bought through the App Store (or Google Play, for the Android app). Apple or Google takes the payment under its own privacy policy: the game never sees your name, email, card or store account. The game keeps only a note of which unlocks you have, on the device. Restore Purchases asks the App Store or Google Play for them again.

Every purchase, and Restore Purchases, starts with the grown-up check. If your child's account needs your approval to buy (Ask to Buy on Apple, purchase approvals in Google Family Link), that still applies.

## Super Fan

Super Fan supports a partner club: before you buy, the game states the share of what we receive for each Super Fan unlock that goes to the club. We count each club's unlocks from the sales reports Apple and Google send us. Those reports say how many of each unlock sold and what we were paid for them, not who bought them. Neither we nor the club learn who you are.

## Going online

Once loaded, the game plays offline. When it is online it fetches only its own files, from the site it came from, to load and to update itself. The app has its files built in, and updates come through the App Store or Google Play.

Its two typefaces come with the game, on the web and in the app. It never loads fonts from Google Fonts or any other site.

## Import

Import is part of Super Fan, in the app only. A grown-up check comes first. Import then fetches the club or tournament page you paste and, if it needs them, up to two pages that page links to on the same site (its table and fixtures). That site receives those requests and your device's internet address, as any website you visit does; nothing about you or your child is sent with them. Import keeps only the team and competition details it needs, such as team names and fixtures, on your device. It never keeps any person's name, photo or contact details.

## The grown-up check

Anything that leaves the game or costs money first asks a grown-up to answer a multiplication written out in words: a purchase, Restore Purchases, Get the App, the privacy and support pages, installing the web version and importing a club page. After two wrong answers in a row it waits before it takes another, so tapping at random does not get past it.

## Your information

The game holds nothing about you anywhere but on your own device, so there is nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. To remove everything it keeps, delete the app or clear the site's data in your browser.
