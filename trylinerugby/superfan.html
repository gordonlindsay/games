PrivacySupportSuper Fan

Super Fan is a one-off unlock in the Try Line Rugby app for supporters of a partner club. Part of every Super Fan unlock goes to that club.

What You Get

  • Everything in Full Game: Championship, Career, Create Team and Hard.
  • Import: your club and its league, from your club's web page or a tournament link.
  • Your club's kit and name on your Super Fan badge.
  • Your club first in Career and Championship.
