Super Fan

PrivacySupportSuper Fan

Super Fan is a one-off unlock in the Tryline Rugby Sevens app for supporters of a partner club. A stated share of what we receive for every Super Fan unlock goes to that club.

What You Get

  • Everything in Full Game: Career, Championship and International modes, Create Team, Hard and the Legends sides.
  • Add Your Team: your club and its league, from your club's web page or a tournament link.
  • Your club's colours on the home screen, and its name on your Super Fan badge.
  • A gold Super Fan trophy for your club, in your Locker and your Trophy Cabinet.
  • Your club first in Career and Championship.

Partner Clubs

Partner clubs appear here, each with its share, once the club has agreed in writing.

How the Share Works

Each partner club has its own Super Fan unlock in the App Store. Apple reports how many of each one sold and what it paid us for them. The club's share is a percentage of what Apple pays us for each unlock, after Apple's commission and VAT, worked out from those reports. It is paid to the club every three months.

The reports count sales, not people. Neither we nor the club learn who bought Super Fan.

Supporters' Wall

Grown-up Super Fans can ask for their name to go on their club's wall, here on this page. It is only ever your choice, it is only for people aged 18 or over, and the game itself sends nothing.

  1. In the app, open the Locker and tap Join the Supporters' Wall beside your Super Fan trophy, or tap Settings, then Grown-Ups.
  2. Answer the grown-up check, and confirm you are 18 or over.
  3. Type your name as you would like it to appear: a first name and your town, or a first name and an initial.
  4. Tap Continue. Your own email app opens with the request written out to support@signcheck.ai. Read it, then send it.
  5. We check each request by hand, then add the name under your club here.

We keep only the name and the club, to show them here. To have your name taken off, email support@signcheck.ai at any time and we will remove it from this page. This page is published from a public code repository on GitHub, so its earlier versions, with any name that was on them, can still be read in that repository's history after a name comes off the page.
