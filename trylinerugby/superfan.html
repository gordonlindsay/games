Super Fan

PrivacySupportSuper Fan

Super Fan is a one-off unlock in the Tryline Rugby Sevens app for supporters of a partner club. A stated share of what we receive for every Super Fan unlock goes to that club.

What You Get

  • Everything in Full Game: Championship, Career, Create Team and Hard.
  • Import: your club and its league, from your club's web page or a tournament link.
  • Your club's kit and name on your Super Fan badge.
  • Your club first in Career and Championship.

Partner Clubs

Partner clubs appear here, each with its share, once the club has agreed in writing.

How the Share Works

Each partner club has its own Super Fan unlock in the App Store. Apple reports how many of each one sold and what it paid us for them. The club's share is a percentage of what Apple pays us for each unlock, after Apple's commission and VAT, worked out from those reports. It is paid to the club [OWNER: how often, for example every three months].

The reports count sales, not people. Neither we nor the club learn who bought Super Fan.
