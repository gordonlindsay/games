# Grassroots to the top: competitions research

Research brief for connecting Try Line Rugby's grassroots clubs to the wider English game. Written 9 November 2026. It changes no game code.

**How to read the labels**

- **Confirmed:** from a primary document, or from independent sources that agree.
- **Reported:** from one secondary source, usually Wikipedia. Good enough to design with, but check it before it appears on screen as fact.
- **Unconfirmed:** the sources conflict or are incomplete.

The RFU's own regulation pages refused automated reading and wouldn't load in the in-app browser, so Regulation 6 and its appendices were not read. An RFU person or a manual read of those pages would settle most items marked Reported or Unconfirmed.

## The main finding

Written against an earlier build, where every Local club carried its real league and `CLUB_RATED` gave each league a rating band, and the career ran Minis, Juniors, County, Pro Club and International. Main's build has club seasons and 12 Local clubs without league data instead; What's built says what that changed. Either way, a league title was the only club trophy below the top.

The design rule that follows from the 2026 RFU vote: **your club can climb as far as the Champ, but only you can reach the Prem.**

### Moving between levels

| Between | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Prem and Champ | No automatic movement from 2026-27. Clubs apply, and must first complete a full Champ season. Relegation only happens if a stronger, viable replacement exists. The Prem plans to reach 12 clubs by 2029-30. | Confirmed ([Cornish Times](https://www.cornish-times.co.uk/sport/rugby/premiership-to-adopt-franchise-style-entry-after-rfu-vote-883769)) |
| Champ and National League 1 | 14th goes down and the National League 1 champions come up. 12th plays 13th, and the loser meets the National League 1 runner-up for the last place. | Reported ([2026-27 Champ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026%E2%80%9327_Champ_Rugby)) |
| Regional 1 | Champions go up. 2nd plays 3rd for a place in an accession final. The loser of 9th v 10th plays in another accession final. The bottom two go down. | Reported ([Regional 1 South Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_1_South_Central)) |
| Regional 2 | Champions go up. 2nd to 5th have promotion play-offs, and 10th and 11th have relegation play-offs. | Reported ([Regional 2 South Central](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_2_South_Central), [South East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_2_South_East)) |
| Counties 5 Surrey | Winners go up. No relegation, and new clubs can apply to join. | Reported ([Counties 5 Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_5_Surrey)) |

**A conflict to note.** The 2026-27 Champ page still says the champions play the Prem's bottom club. Reports of the vote say that play-off has gone, and I've followed the reports.

### County cups (the best fit for grassroots)

Every county sorts its clubs differently, so a county's cup comes from its own table, not a national rule. These tiers are **Reported** from Wikipedia and may predate the 2022 restructure.

| County | Cups and the levels they cover |
|---|---|
| [Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrey_Rugby_Football_Union) | Cup 5, Trophy 6 to 8, Shield 9 and 10, Bowl 11 and 12 |
| [Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampshire_Rugby_Football_Union) | Cup 6 and 7, Bowl 8 and 9, Plate 9 to 11 |
| [Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_Rugby_Football_Union) | Cup 5 and 6, Shield 7 and 8, Vase 9 and 10. Plate for first-round losers from the Cup and Shield, Salver for first-round losers from the Vase. |
| [Middlesex](https://middlesexrugby.com/competitions/middlesex-cup/) | Cup for its top eight clubs, [Bowl](https://middlesexrugby.com/?p=819) for the next 16 |
| [Yorkshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire_Cup_(rugby_union)) | Cup, with the Shield and Trophy below it |
| [Cornwall](https://www.crfu.co.uk/competitions/tribute-cornwall-clubs-cup-competition/) | Clubs Cup, open to clubs that play no league rugby |
| [Sussex](https://sussexrugby.co.uk/competion-regulations) | Regulations exist, but the cup list wasn't published on the page I read |

**Useful for the game:**

- **Plates.** Kent sends first-round losers to a Plate, and the women's cups do the same ([RFU briefing](https://dwrugby.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/Womens-season-briefing-2025_26.pdf)). This guarantees a small club at least two cup matches.
- **History.** The Surrey Cup dates from 1890 and the Kent Cup from 1891. Old cups give small clubs a sense of history.

### Drawn cup ties

The rule varies. [Dorset & Wilts 2025-26](https://dwrugby.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/Cup-Regs-25.6.pdf) (**Confirmed** for that county) uses 10 minutes each way of extra time, then most tries, then most goals from tries, then the away side. [Lancashire](https://lancashirerugby.rfu.club/Media/LancashireCB/Lancashire%20Cups/Adult%20Lancashire%20Cups%20Rules.pdf) (**Reported**) allows joint winners in its final. I couldn't read the RFU's own cup regulations.

The engine can already help. Arcade has sudden-death extra time (`src/arcade.js:9`), switched off for career matches by `goldenOn`, and a level score after it stands as a draw. A cup tie could use that, then most tries, then the away side.

### County Championship

Counties, not clubs: three divisions of 12, 8 and 8, with finals at Twickenham since 1984 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Championship_(rugby_union))). It's played in May and June, at the end of the club season. Devon had group games on 9 and 16 May, then a semi-final, with the final on 6 or 7 June 2026 ([Devon RFU](https://devonrfu.co.uk/?p=916)). That timing means a county call-up can follow a club season rather than replace it.

Recent finals: Kent beat Yorkshire in 2025 and 2024, and Lancashire in 2023. **Cornwall beat Lancashire 39-24 in 2026** ([Forces News](https://www.forcesnews.com/sport/rugby-union/sam-matavesi-captains-cornwall-county-championship-triumph-allianz-stadium)).

**Unconfirmed:** who is eligible, which division each county is in now, and when Hampshire's 63-7 win over Surrey at Twickenham took place ([Rugby Paper index](https://therugbypaper.co.uk/all/domestic-club-rugby-union/bill-beaumont-cup)).

## Age groups

| Fact | Status |
|---|---|
| Mixed rugby stops at U12, which matches `MIXED_THROUGH` in the game. | Confirmed ([Regulation 15, May 2024 edition](https://www.bororugby.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Regulation-15-2024-25.pdf)) |
| From 17, players can play adult rugby with club and county approval, but not in the front row of a contested scrum until 18. | Confirmed (same) |
| U18s and below can't play in the Prem, Champ or their cups, except in exceptional cases with approval. | Confirmed (same) |
| Sussex boys play "waterfall" competitions at U12 to U14, leagues at U15 to Colts, and a Colts Cup. Girls have waterfalls at U12 and U14, leagues at U16 and U18, and cups at U14, U16 and U18. | Reported ([Sussex RFU](https://sussexrugby.co.uk/competion-regulations)) |
| Middlesex U13 and U14 boys no longer play in leagues. The page is undated, and its latest dated content is from 2024-25. | Reported ([Middlesex RFU](https://middlesexrugby.com/?p=3073)) |
| A national rule on the youngest age for leagues or cups. | Unconfirmed: I read the 2024 edition, not the May 2025 one, and its Competition Menu is a separate document |

So the Minis league table and promotion aren't realistic, and Juniors probably fit better as a waterfall, then a league. A waterfall seems to mean losers drop into a lower bracket, but no source defined it, so check that first.

**Route to the pros (Reported):** Prem academies lead player development, with an [U15 identification festival at Exeter](https://exeterchiefs.co.uk/academy/dpp-programme). A [National Schools Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RFU_National_Schools_Cup) and a [national Colts cup](https://dorkingrfc.com/news/celebrating-the-2024-colts-graduates?page=4) also exist.

## The women's game

Female careers use the same clubs through `womenClub`, but the real structure has six levels rather than eleven. All **Confirmed** for 2025-26 by the [RFU briefing](https://dwrugby.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/Womens-season-briefing-2025_26.pdf), except the top level.

| Level | League | Leagues | Clubs each |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Premiership Women's Rugby (by licence; Reported, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_rugby_union_system)) | 1 | not checked |
| 2 | Championship 1 North, South | 2 | up to 10 |
| 3 | Championship 2 | 4 | up to 10 |
| 4 | National Challenge 1 | 8 | up to 10 |
| 5 | National Challenge 2 | 10 | up to 10 |
| 6 | National Challenge 3 | 21 | 6 to 10 |
| Entry | Inner Warrior Series | not a league | |

- **Intermediate Cup:** Championship 2 and National Challenge 1.
- **Junior Cup:** National Challenge 2 and below, including Inner Warrior Series teams.
- **Plates:** first-round cup losers drop into a matching Plate.
- **Championship Playoff:** the top two from each Championship 1 league.

In 2026, [Ivybridge beat Old Albanian Saints II 40-17](https://plymouthsportsgazette.com/2026/05/25/ivybridge-women-cap-a-dream-season-by-winning-the-rfu-intermediate-cup-at-twickenham/) in the Intermediate Cup final at Twickenham.

**Other data points**

- **Feltham's place.** The game says 10th, and the Counties 5 Surrey page says 8th. Worth checking.
- **Clubs to add.** No level 8 clubs are in the game. [Counties 2 Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counties_2_Surrey) has Chipstead, Old Whitgiftian, Purley John Fisher and Thamesians among others. Sutton & Epsom, reported as the [most successful Surrey Cup club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surrey_Cup), are in Regional 2 South East. Each new club needs real kit colours, and the lists are append-only.
- **Thin cups.** On last season's leagues, the Surrey Cup has 2 clubs, the Trophy 5, the Shield 3 and the Bowl 6. An eight-team knockout needs more clubs, or rivals from the next level up.
- **Champ list.** `ENGLAND_TWO` is 2025-26. The 2026-27 Champ adds Blackheath and Rotherham and no longer has Cambridge.

## What changed from the plan

- **Kent aren't simply the strongest county.** They won from 2023 to 2025, but Cornwall won in 2026. Rate counties from the last few finals, and keep it in one table so it's easy to update.
- **Cups can use existing extra time.** Sudden death is already in the engine.
- **Plates are now part of the recommendation.** They guarantee small clubs more matches at little cost.
- **Club data needs a season label.** Three game clubs have already moved up and one has withdrawn. Ideally a save says which season its club data is from, and any update is an explicit data refresh.
- **Farnham can offer a real choice.** As a joint Surrey and Hampshire member, a Farnham player could pick a county.

## What's built

**In the game (on main's club-season career):** the data spine and county cups. The rest of the table below is still a proposal.

- **County cups in a Local club's year.** After its league, a Local club plays the cup its county runs for its level: Surrey Cup, Trophy, Shield or Bowl, or Hampshire's for Aldershot. Eight sides, Quarter-Finals to a Final. It's the cups engine's `county` kind (`src/cups.js`), so its screens, Golden Try, save and trophy come from the same code as the European Cup's. The data is `src/competitions.js`.
- **Levels and counties are written down, not read.** Main's Local list is 12 Surrey-area clubs with no league data, rated on the professional sides' scale. So each club's level and county is a table taken from the club table above. Quins (the amateur club) is level 11: the club's own 2025-26 fixtures are in Counties 5 Surrey, though Wikipedia says Counties 4.
- **No Plates yet.** A first-round loser goes out. A Plate would mean a second bracket in the cups engine.
- **Rivals come from the Local list.** Aldershot's Hampshire cup draws Surrey clubs, because the game has no other Hampshire clubs.
- **Plain cup names.** A made club plays "County Cup", "County Bowl" and so on.
- **Not ported.** An earlier branch (`docs/grassroots-competitions`, commit `abce6b0`) also had Minis tag festivals, Juniors without scrums, a Club stage and training by stage. Main's career replaced stages with club seasons and has no Minis, so those weren't carried over.

## Recommended connections

| Order | Connection | What it does | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data spine | `src/competitions.js`: each club's level, county and cup tier | Low |
| 2 | County cups and Plates | A knockout beside the league, stored in `cr.cup`, using `simMatch` for other ties | Low to medium |
| 3 | Right county, sensible signing | Aldershot play for Hampshire, and first signings lean English | Low |
| 4 | Home club thread | Your old club's results keep coming, and the Hall of Fame shows where you started | Low |
| 5 | Club stage | Minis, Juniors, **Club**, County, Pro Club, International | High |
| 6 | National cup and play-offs | Counties 1 and below chase a national final; regional clubs get promotion play-offs | Medium |
| Later | Age-group formats, club mode | Festivals and waterfalls; a mode that climbs the pyramid | Medium |

**Traps to avoid**

- **The Club stage's ratings.** Counties 5 is rated 40, after Juniors at up to 55, so the ladder would go backwards. Add a third `CAREER_CUT` entry that maps levels 11 to 5 onto about 52 to 58.
- **Saves.** Inserting a stage shifts stage numbers, so old saves must move up by one. Replace the hard-coded `stage === 2/3/4` checks with stage ids.
- **Career length.** A sixth stage adds seasons, so retune the bars (about 73 for Club) and recheck with `tools/ladder.mjs` and `tools/career.mjs`.
- **The name "Twickenham".** The earlier build used it for a top-flight side (`HAR`), and a [real amateur Twickenham club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional_2_South_Central) plays in Regional 2 South Central. Call the final's venue "the national stadium".
- **Trophy names.** Prefer plain names such as "County Bowl" over "Bill Beaumont Cup" or sponsor names, in line with how the game treats professional clubs. This hasn't been legally checked.
- **Imported clubs.** Their level can probably be read from the league name, but their county can't. Fall back to "County Cup", or ask the player.
- **Women's careers.** Eleven-level men's cups don't map onto the women's structure. Use Intermediate and Junior cups for those careers, or decide to simplify.
