Contact Games (U9 and Up) Learn Real Tackling With a Club Coach. Not One to Try at Home.

For Grown-Ups

These are games first. Each one rewards a real habit, so children pick it up by playing. A short line after each run says what worked.

  • Tag Chase: changing direction to get away, and pulling a tag off a hip with no contact. The youngest age groups play tag rugby.
  • Bouncy Egg: going to meet the ball, with eyes on it until it arrives.
  • Pass Around the Circle: passes that finish at chest height, where they are easiest to catch.
  • Cones: footwork and clean changes of direction.
  • Create a Two-on-One: running at a defender, then passing at the right moment.
  • Kicking: aiming first, then a controlled strike.
  • Defend a Breakaway: getting across to help when a team-mate is beaten. At the Kids level and on Minis it ends with a tag, not a tackle.
  • Tackling: head behind the ball carrier, eyes up, aiming low at the legs and well below the chest, arms round and legs driving.
  • Driving: a low, strong body shape with the hips lower than the shoulders, and timing the push.
  • Ball Back: a safe two-handed hold, falling without an arm out to stop the fall, and getting the ball back to the team.

Contact starts at under-9 with the tackle only. Under-9s have no scrums and pass the ball on when tackled, so Driving and the placing in Ball Back go further than under-9s play. Real contact skills need a club coach and planned, supervised sessions. The Kids level and Minis hide Contact Games, and only a grown-up can leave the Kids level. The game never asks for an age.

Head knocks: if anyone may have a concussion, they stop playing straight away and see a doctor or call NHS 111 within a day. No sport for at least 24 hours, and no match for at least 21 days, with a phased return in between.

How to Play

Stick
Touch anywhere on the left half and drag. Near a tackler, flick it sideways to Side Step. Pull it back and push on for a Dummy.
Pass
A tap goes to the teammate in the ring. White means they are free; the ring tints when a defender is on them. Tilt the stick toward a side. Passes can't go forward. Hold the button and the ring turns gold: the ball skips the nearest player and floats to the next one out. A tint inside the gold means that player is marked too.
Kick
Hold to power, release to kick. A tap is a chip.
Goal Kicks
Steer the arrow. Hold Kick, let go in the gold.
Drop Goal
In range the small button reads Drop Goal. Hold it, steer, let go in the gold before they reach you.
Tackle
When they have the ball. It lights up when you can reach them. Miss and you're on the floor.
Sprint
Hold it for a burst of speed, with the ball or without. The ring drains while you hold and refills when you let go.
Offload
Press Pass as you're tackled, when it glows.
Switch
The dashed ring shows who. Nearest the ball, or the next one if that is you. If your player is behind the ball, the nearest teammate still in front, and the button pulses.
Ruck
The ring moves to the teammate who will pick the ball up. Place them with the stick, or let go and they run to the ball. Pass, Kick and Tackle stay dead until it is out.
Duels
Act on the gold ring: flick the stick to step, or press the button. In a Key Pass, Dummy when they drift to your runner. Each one starts in slow motion.
Hints
The card in the top corner names the control for each close-up. Untick Hints on it, or set Match Help to Off on the menu.
Tutorial
The first time each close-up comes up, play pauses and a card says what to do. Replay Tutorial below shows them all again.
The Ball
A white bubble with a ball in it stands over whoever has the ball, on either side, and an arrow at the edge points to it when it is off the screen. On the grass, rays mean it is loose. In your first matches a sign under the clock says Your Ball, Stop Them or Get the Ball. In a career the star is your player and the ring is who you control now.
Coach
The first time Pass, Tackle, an offload, Sprint or a side step comes up, one line tells you. The menu’s Match Help switch controls these tips and the control hints together.
Scrum
Press Push on the call of Set for a good start, then tap it fast. Out-shove theirs to steal it.
Lineout
Press Jump so your jumper is at full height as the ball arrives. Beat theirs to steal the throw.
Keyboard
Arrows or WASD to run: tap across near a tackler to Side Step, tap back for a Dummy. J Pass (hold for a long pass) / Offload / Tackle / Kick. K Kick / Switch / Drop Goal. L Sprint. P Pause.
Controller
Stick or D-Pad to run: flick across near a tackler to Side Step, pull back and push on for a Dummy. Bottom Button Pass / Tackle. Right Button Kick / Switch. Shoulder Sprint. Start Pause. Press any button to connect it.
2 Players
Player 1: W A S D Run, F Pass, G Kick, H Sprint. Player 2: Arrows Run, / Pass, . Kick, , Sprint. Pass is also Tackle, the goal kick (hold it), Push and Jump; Kick is also Switch, Drop Goal and Dummy. A controller takes a seat with one press. In a duel the other player guards, drifts or steps with their run keys before the ring opens, or presses Kick for a Dummy as the passer.

Install & Offline
On iPhone or iPad, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Other browsers may offer Install in their menu. Visit online first and wait for Offline Ready on the home menu before playing without a connection. Browser storage must remain available. Updates appear on the home menu and wait for you to choose Update Game.
Saving
This browser keeps your settings, the teams you make, and a championship's fixtures, table and round. Continue Championship picks up the next match. Start Fresh on the table clears the current championship and keeps completed seasons in the Trophy Cabinet. Leaving a match, or reloading, keeps the same round to play again.
