    Contact Games (U9 and Up) Learn Real Tackling With a Club Coach. Not One to Try at Home.

    For Grown-Ups

    These are games first. Each one rewards a real habit, so children pick it up by playing. A short line after each run says what worked.

    • Tag Chase: changing direction to get away, and pulling a tag off a hip with no contact. The youngest age groups play tag rugby.
    • Bouncy Egg: going to meet the ball, with eyes on it until it arrives.
    • Pass Around the Circle: passes that finish at chest height, where they are easiest to catch.
    • Cones: footwork and clean changes of direction.
    • Create a Gap: running at a defender, then passing at the right moment, with more players a side as the rounds go on.
    • Kicking: aiming first, then a controlled strike.
    • Defend a Breakaway: getting across to help when a team-mate is beaten, and reading a support runner. At the Kids level and on Minis it ends with a tag, not a tackle.
    • Tackling: head behind the ball carrier, eyes up, aiming low at the legs and well below the chest, arms round and legs driving.
    • Driving: a low, strong body shape with the hips lower than the shoulders, and timing the push.
    • Ball Back: a safe two-handed hold, falling without an arm out to stop the fall, and getting the ball back to the team.

    Contact starts at under-9 with the tackle only. Under-9s have no scrums and pass the ball on when tackled, so Driving and the placing in Ball Back go further than under-9s play. Real contact skills need a club coach and planned, supervised sessions. The Kids level and Minis hide Contact Games, and only a grown-up can leave the Kids level. The game asks an age group (Mini, Junior or Adult) and a gender, each one skippable, to set its level and side, and keeps them on this device. Mini turns on the Kids level and Minis.

    Head knocks: if anyone may have a concussion, they stop playing straight away and see a doctor or call NHS 111 within a day. No sport for at least 24 hours, and no match for at least 21 days, with a phased return in between.
