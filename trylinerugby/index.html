How to Play

Stick
Touch anywhere on the left half and drag. Flick it sideways near a tackler to step him.
Pass
Goes to the teammate in the white ring. Tilt the stick up or down to pick a side. Passes can't go forward.
Kick
Hold to power, release to kick. A tap is a chip. Through the posts in open play is a drop goal.
Tackle
When they have the ball. Dive at the carrier from close range. Miss and you're on the floor.
Switch
Take the defender nearest the ball.
Duels
Act on the gold ring: flick the stick to step, or press the button. In a Key Pass, Dummy when he drifts to your runner.
Scrum
Press Push on the call of Set for a good hit, then tap it fast with the stick held forward. Out-shove theirs to steal it.
Lineout
Press Jump so your jumper is at full height as the ball arrives. Beat theirs to steal the throw.
Keyboard
Arrows or WASD to run. J Pass / Tackle. K Kick / Switch. P Pause.
