Restore Purchases

Bought Full Game or Super Fan before, on this phone or another, and it is locked now? The game asks the store for your unlocks each time it starts online, so they often come back by themselves. If not, get them back for free:

  1. Open Tryline Rugby Sevens.
  2. On the home screen, tap Settings, then Grown-Ups.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.
  4. Answer the grown-up check.

Use the same Apple Account, or the same Google account, that made the purchase. The App Store may ask you to sign in.

Bought on your iPhone, playing on your child's?

With Apple's Family Sharing, and purchase sharing turned on, everyone in your family group gets the unlocks on their own Apple Account. Open the game on their iPhone: the unlocks come in as it starts, or with Restore Purchases. There is no need to sign their iPhone in to your Apple Account, which would take Ask to Buy off it and put your payment details on it.

For Parents

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. It is a cartoon rugby game: tackles are part of the sport, with no blood and no injuries shown. There is a Kids level made for 6 to 10 year olds.

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads of any kind.

Does it collect information about my child?

No. Everything the game keeps stays on the device, and in your phone's own backup if you use one; we never receive any of it. There is no account, no sign-in and no tracking. The Privacy page has the detail.

Can my child talk to other players?

No. There is no chat, and no way to contact anyone through the game.

Can my child's name appear anywhere?

No. With Super Fan, a grown-up aged 18 or over can ask for their own name to go on the club's Supporters' Wall, behind the grown-up check, by sending us an email from their own email app. We check every request by hand and never list a child. The Super Fan page has the steps.

Does the Kids level change?

Yes, a little at a time. It gets harder as your child wins and easier after a draw or a loss, and it always stays easier than Easy. The phone keeps one Kids level, so brothers and sisters share it. For a younger child, or a fresh start, tap Grown-Ups, then Reset Kids Level, which shows once the level has moved.

Can my child buy things by accident?

Every purchase starts with a grown-up check: a multiplication written out in words that a young child cannot easily answer, though an older child may work it out. Ask to Buy on Apple, or purchase approvals in Google Family Link, still apply on top. To turn off in-app purchases altogether on your child's iPhone, use Screen Time's Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Does it need the internet?

It plays offline. Buying an unlock, Restore Purchases and Add Your Team need a connection.

How do I remove everything it keeps?

Delete the app. On the web, clear the browser's data for the game's site. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account, so Restore Purchases brings them back later.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
